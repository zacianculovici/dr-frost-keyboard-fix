iPad Safari helper

Dr Frost Maths keyboard fix

Add this bookmarklet to Safari, then tap it on a Dr Frost Maths algebra question to type answers with your iPad keyboard.

Drag this to your bookmarks bar

On iPad, touch and hold the link, then choose to add it to your bookmarks.

How to use it

  1. Add the blue link to your bookmarks.
  2. Open an algebra question on drfrost.org.
  3. Tap the bookmark and then tap the answer box.

The bookmarklet only runs on the Dr Frost Maths answer page. Reloading the page removes the fix, so tap the bookmark again.

Shortcuts: type ^ for superscript, or use Option/Alt + Shift + 6 for square root.
